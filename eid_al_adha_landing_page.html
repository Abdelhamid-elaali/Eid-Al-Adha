The Heart of the Occasion

Four pillars of Eid al-Adha

🕌

Prayer

Gathering at dawn for communal Eid prayer, a powerful moment of unity and devotion across the Muslim world.

🌙

Sacrifice

Commemorating Ibrahim's devotion through the ritual of Qurbani — an act of submission and remembrance.

🤝

Giving

Distributing meat generously — one-third kept, one-third shared with neighbours, one-third given to those in need.

❤️

Togetherness

Families and communities gathering, sharing meals, exchanging warm wishes, and strengthening bonds.

How We Celebrate

Traditions across the world

01

New Clothes

Wearing new or finest clothes is a cherished tradition that symbolises renewal and joy as families step out for morning prayers.

02

Festive Feasts

Tables brimming with slow-cooked lamb, fragrant rice, sweet pastries, and traditional dishes passed down through generations.

03

Visiting Loved Ones

Travelling across cities and countries to be with family, exchange warm Eid greetings, and share in the blessings of the day.

04

Charitable Acts

Going beyond Qurbani — volunteering, donating, and helping neighbours as an expression of gratitude for one's blessings.
